# Supplementary tables

The supplementary tables of the paper (Tables S1, S2, ...) as CSV files,
`S<nn>_<method>_<content>.csv`. `S<nn>` is the number of the table (Table S1, S2, ...,
numbered in the order of their first citation in the paper; zero-padded in the file names
so that they sort in order); tables that span the methods have no `<method>`.

The tables and what each one lists:

<!-- tables:begin -->
- **S1** (`S01_expected_density_polarities.csv`): All methods: expected density factor of each polarity of Φ. GreedyMini-based schemes, counted by the selection code of each method on 10^9 uniformly random bases, a different string for each of the five seeds. Columns: the method, k and w; the scheme and its order; the polarity of Φ (at0-cg1: {A,T} mapped to 0; at1-cg0: {A,T} mapped to 1); the expected density factor (mean and standard deviation over the seeds); and whether the polarity is the one reported in the paper (at0-cg1, chosen arbitrarily).
- **S2** (`S02_particular_density.csv`): All methods: particular density factor of each scheme on each dataset. Columns: the method, dataset, k and w; the scheme and its order; and the particular density factor (mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds; the default schemes of Minimap2 and KMC3 have no seed). The GreedyMini orders are built as in Table 2 of the paper, with {A,T} mapped to 0 by Φ. Minimap2 uses a GreedyMini order on t-mers in the mod-minimizer only with k=19 (CHM13 chrX, GRCh38).
- **S3** (`S03_polarity_comparison.csv`): All methods: the two polarities of Φ. Each metric of the GreedyMini-based schemes on each dataset, with {A,T} mapped to 0 (at0-cg1, reported in the paper) and to 1 (at1-cg0). Columns: the method, dataset, k and w; the scheme and its order; the metric, with its unit; its mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds with each polarity; the ratio at1-cg0 / at0-cg1 (geometric mean over the paired seeds) and its 95% confidence interval; the Bonferroni-corrected p-value of the paired t-test on the log ratios (multiplied by the number of GreedyMini-based schemes on the dataset and metric); and the verdict as in the significance tests (lower or higher if significant, else equivalent within ±5% or inconclusive). Minimap2 with the occurrence threshold of the default scheme; KMC3 with the binary table.
- **S4** (`S04_significance_runtime.csv`): All methods: significance tests of the runtime. Change of each runtime metric of the figures from the default scheme (Methods). Columns: the method, metric, dataset, k and w; the scheme and its order; the ratio of the metric to that of the default scheme (geometric mean over the 5 paired seeds) and its 95% confidence interval; the Bonferroni-corrected p-value of the paired t-test on the log ratios (multiplied by the number of schemes compared with the default scheme on the dataset and metric); and the verdict: lower or higher (significant, adjusted p<0.05), equivalent (90% confidence interval within ±5%), or inconclusive.
- **S5** (`S05_kraken2_build.csv`): Kraken2: database build, from the strain-exclusion library, 16 threads. Columns: the scheme and its order; the particular density factor; the distinct selected k-mers (millions); the build wall time and its two steps: estimating the capacity of the hash table, and processing the library (computing the selected k-mers of every sequence and inserting them into the table), which with 1 to 2 s of the other steps of kraken2-build sum to the build wall time; the build CPU time; the build peak memory; and the database size. Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds.
- **S6** (`S06_kraken2_classification.csv`): Kraken2: classification, one thread, minimum hit groups 2 (the default of Kraken2). Columns: the dataset; the scheme and its order; the processing time (as timed by Kraken2: classifying the reads, without loading the database); the total wall time, which is the sum of the processing time and the database load time; the database load time; the classification peak memory; the classified reads (% of the reads); and the speed (million reads per minute of processing time). Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds. The load time, and with it the total time, depends on whether the database was in the page cache: over the 105 runs, it took 0.9 to 3.4 s in 95 runs, and, when read from disk, 6.3 to 16.4 s in 10 runs.
- **S7** (`S07_kraken2_classification_profile.csv`): Kraken2: classification profile, minimum hit groups 2 (Appendix (Profiling)). The classification of the reads has three timed steps: computing the selected k-mer of every L-window, looking the selected k-mers up in the hash table, and resolving the hits into a taxon; they take 57% to 76% of the processing time (Table S6), and the rest is reading the reads and writing the classification of each read. Columns: the dataset; the scheme and its order; the shares of the three steps (% of the time of the three steps, summing to 100); the time of computing the selected k-mers; the lookups per Mbp of reads; the lookups that miss the table (% of the lookups); the time per lookup; and, for the GreedyMini orders, the k-mers of the reads whose rank is infinite, i.e., outside the UHS (% of the k-mers). Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds.
- **S8** (`S08_minimap2_build_mapping_profile.csv`): Minimap2: index build and mapping profile (Appendix (Profiling)), with the occurrence threshold fixed to that of the default scheme. Columns: the dataset, k and w; the scheme and its order. Index build (the runs of Table S9, 16 threads): the time until Minimap2 has collected the selected k-mers of the reference (from its log; reading the reference and computing its selected k-mers, mostly in one thread) and its share of the build wall time, the rest being sorting the selected k-mers and building the hash tables; and the build CPU time. Mapping (a separate profiling run with one thread, on the first 3,000 reads of each dataset, all 1,000 for the ONT reads): the time of its four steps, which sum to the profiled mapping time: computing the selected k-mers of the reads, looking up the seeds in the index, chaining the anchors, and aligning the chains. Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds. And, for seed 0 of the scheme, the seeds per kb of reads and the anchors per read (thousands).
- **S9** (`S09_minimap2_build_mapping.csv`): Minimap2: index build and mapping, 16 threads, with the occurrence threshold fixed to the value of the default scheme or at its default, which Minimap2 derives from each index. Columns: the block (dataset and threshold setting), k and w; the scheme and its order; the occurrence threshold; the particular density factor; the index build wall time; the mapping processing time (the mapping wall time minus the index load time that Minimap2 reports); the peak memory of the index build and of the mapping; and the index size. Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds (the default scheme has no seed).
- **S10** (`S10_kmc3_density_correlations.csv`): KMC3: correlation with the particular density. How closely each metric follows the particular density factor (Methods). Every run of a scheme is paired with the run of the default scheme of the same dataset, setting and seed, and gives the ratios of its particular density factor and of its metric to those of the default scheme. Columns: the metric; the Pearson correlation r of the two ratios over the runs of all datasets, and the number of paired runs; and r over the runs of each dataset.
- **S11** (`S11_kmc3_counting.csv`): KMC3: counting, L=28, 1024 bins, 24 threads. Columns: the dataset; the scheme and its order; the particular density factor; the superwindows (millions); the mean superwindow length (L-mers per superwindow); the time of stage 1 (computing the selected k-mers and distributing the superwindows into bins) and of stage 2 (counting the L-mers of each bin), as reported by KMC3, which sum to the total wall time; the total wall time; the CPU time; and the temporary disk used by the bins. Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds (the default scheme has no seed). The peak memory is not listed, because the memory of KMC3 is limited by design (12 GB, 32 GB for H. sapiens 3) and is the same for all schemes.
- **S12** (`S12_kmc3_order_cost_profile.csv`): KMC3: order cost profile (Appendix (Profiling)), on one core, on 200 Mbp of the reads of each dataset. Columns: the dataset; the scheme and its order; the time of ranking every k-mer (ns per base); the time of ranking every k-mer and selecting the k-mer of each L-window (ns per base), which includes the ranking; its ratio to that of the default order; and the superwindows per kb of reads. Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds, each the median of 7 repetitions.
- **S13** (`S13_sshash_build.csv`): SSHash: index build, k=21, w=11, 16 threads. Columns: the collection; the scheme and its order (the GreedyMini order built for 15-mers, center-extended to 21-mers); the particular density factor; the distinct selected k-mers (millions); the build wall time; the build peak memory; and the index size, in bits per L-mer and in GB. Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds.
- **S14** (`S14_sshash_streaming_query_profile.csv`): SSHash: streaming query profile (Appendix (Profiling)), one thread. Each L-mer of the reads is either absent from the collection, or present and found by a lookup, or present and found by extending the previous L-mer along the stored string, without a lookup; these three shares sum to 100. A lookup is either a full lookup of the bucket of the selected k-mer or, when the selected k-mer is that of the previous lookup, a check of the positions of that bucket, which are cached. Columns: the collection; the scheme and its order; the L-mers absent, found by a lookup, and found by extension (% of the L-mers); the full lookups and the cached checks (% of the L-mers); the positions of the selected k-mer scanned per full lookup; and the processing time per L-mer. Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds.
- **S15** (`S15_sshash_lookups_queries.csv`): SSHash: isolated lookups and streaming queries, one thread. Columns: the collection; the scheme and its order; the time of an isolated lookup of an L-mer present in the collection (positive) and of one absent from it (negative), and of accessing an L-mer by its identifier, in ns per L-mer; and the processing time of the streaming query of the real reads (as timed by SSHash, without loading the index). Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds.
- **S16** (`S16_significance_space.csv`): All methods: significance tests of the memory and storage usage. Change of each space metric of the figures from the default scheme (Methods). Columns: the method, metric, dataset, k and w; the scheme and its order; the ratio of the metric to that of the default scheme (geometric mean over the 5 paired seeds) and its 95% confidence interval; the Bonferroni-corrected p-value of the paired t-test on the log ratios (multiplied by the number of schemes compared with the default scheme on the dataset and metric); and the verdict: lower or higher (significant, adjusted p<0.05), equivalent (90% confidence interval within ±5%), or inconclusive.
- **S17** (`S17_kraken2_density_correlations.csv`): Kraken2: correlation with the particular density. How closely each metric follows the particular density factor (Methods). Every run of a scheme is paired with the run of the default scheme of the same dataset, setting and seed, and gives the ratios of its particular density factor and of its metric to those of the default scheme. Columns: the metric; the Pearson correlation r of the two ratios over the runs of all datasets, and the number of paired runs; and r over the runs of each dataset. The database is built once, from the strain-exclusion library, so its metrics have no per-dataset values; the classification is given for each dataset, at minimum hit groups 2.
- **S18** (`S18_minimap2_density_correlations.csv`): Minimap2: correlation with the particular density. How closely each metric follows the particular density factor (Methods). Every run of a scheme is paired with the run of the default scheme of the same dataset, setting and seed, and gives the ratios of its particular density factor and of its metric to those of the default scheme. Columns: the metric; the Pearson correlation r of the two ratios over the runs of all datasets, and the number of paired runs; and r over the runs of each dataset. Only the runs with the occurrence threshold fixed to the value of the default scheme are paired, since the threshold derived from each index differs between schemes.
- **S19** (`S19_sshash_density_correlations.csv`): SSHash: correlation with the particular density. How closely each metric follows the particular density factor (Methods). Every run of a scheme is paired with the run of the default scheme of the same dataset, setting and seed, and gives the ratios of its particular density factor and of its metric to those of the default scheme. Columns: the metric; the Pearson correlation r of the two ratios over the runs of all datasets, and the number of paired runs; and r over the runs of each dataset.
- **S20** (`S20_sshash_buckets.csv`): SSHash: buckets. A bucket holds the positions of one selected k-mer. Columns: the collection; the scheme and its order; the buckets (millions); the occurrences per selected k-mer (positions per bucket); and the L-mers in singleton, light and heavy buckets (% of the L-mers, summing to 100). Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds.
- **S21** (`S21_sshash_index_space_breakdown.csv`): SSHash: index space breakdown, mean over the 5 seeds. Columns: the collection; the scheme and its order; the index size (bits per L-mer); the bits per L-mer saved relative to the default scheme (negative: the index grew); and the share of the saved bits of each structure (% of the saved bits, summing to 100): the tags of the selected k-mers, the minimal perfect hash function (MPHF), the strings and their offsets, the skew index, and the rest; a negative share is a structure that grew.
- **S22** (`S22_kraken2_accuracy_strain_exclusion.csv`): Kraken2: accuracy on the strain-exclusion reads. Simulated reads (Mason, held-out genomes), minimum hit groups 2 (the default of Kraken2). Columns: the block (genome group and rank); the scheme and its order; and the sensitivity, the positive predictive value (PPV) and their harmonic mean F1, all in %, as the mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds.
- **S23** (`S23_kraken2_accuracy_cami.csv`): Kraken2: accuracy on the CAMI II reads. Simulated reads, minimum hit groups 2. Columns: the block (genome group and rank); the scheme and its order; and the sensitivity, the positive predictive value (PPV) and their harmonic mean F1, all in %, as the mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds.
- **S24** (`S24_kraken2_accuracy_fda_argos.csv`): Kraken2: accuracy on the FDA-ARGOS reads. Genus-level calls of each of the 25 FDA-ARGOS experiments, scored separately against the SRA-assigned taxon, at minimum hit groups 2, the default of Kraken2. Columns: the block (SRA run and species); the scheme and its order; and the reads (% of the reads of the experiment, summing to 100) called as the assigned genus or a descendant (concordant), off its lineage (discordant), as an ancestor of the genus (ancestor), or unclassified, as the mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds.
- **S25** (`S25_minimap2_accuracy_simulated.csv`): Minimap2: accuracy on the simulated reads, with the occurrence threshold fixed to that of the default scheme. Columns: the block (dataset and threshold); the scheme and its order; the reads (% of the reads) mapped, mapped correctly (the primary alignment on the true contig, overlapping the true interval by at least 10% of their union), and mapped wrongly (mapped but not correctly), so that mapped = correct + wrong; the reads from repeats mapped correctly (% of the reads from repeats); the reads mapped correctly with an overlap of at least 50% (% of the reads); and the mean identity of the primary alignments (%, 1 minus the per-base divergence). Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds (the default scheme has no seed).
- **S26** (`S26_minimap2_accuracy_ont.csv`): Minimap2: accuracy on the ONT reads: 1,000 real reads of CHM13 rel3 mapped to GRCh38, without truth, with the occurrence threshold fixed to that of the default scheme. Columns: the block (dataset and threshold); the scheme and its order; the mapped reads (% of the reads); and the mean identity of the primary alignments (%, 1 minus the per-base divergence). Mean and standard deviation over the 5 seeds (the default scheme has no seed).

Each file has the rows of its table, laid out as the benchmark workbooks are:

- `block`: the dataset or setting of the row (the block of the table), where a table has
  several.
- `method`, `metric`, `dataset`: in the tables that span methods, metrics or datasets
  (S01 to S04, S16).
- `k`, `w`: the length of the selected k-mer and the number of k-mers per window, where
  they vary within a table (S01 to S04, S08, S09, S16; Minimap2 uses k = 19 on CHM13 chrX
  and GRCh38 and k = 15 on the ONT reads).
- `scheme`, `order`: the scheme (`minimizer` or `mod-minimizer`) and its order in the
  paper's names (`xor`, `xor-pcg-hash`, `GreedyMini`, or the default order of the method,
  marked `(default)`). The scheme ids of the benchmarks map to this pair as:
  `default`/`native` -> `minimizer` with the default order; `xor`, `xor-pcg-hash`,
  `greedymini` -> `minimizer` with that order; `mod-minimizer-xor`,
  `mod-minimizer-greedymini` -> `mod-minimizer` with `xor` or `GreedyMini`. Every
  GreedyMini-based scheme is given with the polarity of Φ chosen arbitrarily, A,T -> 0
  (`at0-cg1`); S01 and S03 list both polarities (S01 in the column `polarity`, S03 in the
  columns `at0-cg1` and `at1-cg0`).
- `<metric> mean`, `<metric> std`: the mean and the standard deviation over the five
  runs; the standard deviation is empty where it is 0 (a single run, or a value that
  does not vary).
- An empty value means the scheme does not apply (e.g. no mod-minimizer in KMC3).
- Units are in the column names. Shares are percentages, named `(% of ...)` after what they
  are a share of; ratios are named after what they divide. The caption of each table above
  explains every column, and how its times add up.

## How the tables are made

`paper-export/make_tables.py` computes every table from the result sets of the benchmarks, including
the statistical tests; see `paper-export/README.md`. The expected densities (S01, and Table 2 of the
paper) are counted in the result sets by the tools of `benchmark/common/expected_density/`, which run
each method's own selection code on 10^9 random bases per seed.
